About

I'm a product manager who started as an engineer. A CS degree from Mumbai, three peer-reviewed machine learning papers, and an MS in Management Information Systems from Texas A&M sit underneath every roadmap I write — which is why I'm as comfortable in Postman and a Bitbucket pipeline as I am in Figma.

Over the last four years that's meant launching a cross-platform insurance app to 500K+ users, architecting a multi-agent commerce platform as a founding product manager, and — right now — rebuilding the prompting and developer-platform layer behind an AI voice-agent product at HireEZ. The common thread is ambiguous problems, systems that have to hold up in production, and a plan for getting the market to actually adopt them.

Selected work

Four ways the ambiguity got resolved

01 Developer platforms · AI

A developer surface out of a fragmented internal wiki

Read the case study → Ask for a walkthrough ↗
Problem
Sales GTM and Account Management at HireEZ had no fast way to answer "does the product support X" — every question routed through Product and Engineering.
Approach
Built a custom MCP server on top of the internal integration dashboard, querying the master database and the markdown docs engineering keeps current on every major release, to surface live feature-support answers natively inside Claude.
Impact
Turnaround on feature-support questions cut 93%, removing Product/Engineering as a bottleneck for GTM.
02 AI · Agent systems

Teaching agents to run a marketplace

Read the case study → Ask for a walkthrough ↗
Problem
As founding PM at VYBD.AI (formerly BulkMagic), I needed to compress slow human research and manual seller operations into automated systems — with no dedicated infra team.
Approach
Architected a multi-agent AI Market Research Agent, orchestrated a micro-warehousing platform (supplier onboarding, KYC, shipment tracking), and connected agent workflows for catalog automation.
Impact
Decision cycles cut from 6 months to 8 weeks at 85% accuracy; vendor onboarding 60% faster; manual operational gaps down 40%.
03 Zero-to-one · Fintech

Zero to one, at insurance scale

Read the case study → Résumé detail ↗
Problem
Health, motor and travel insurance lived in disconnected legacy flows. ICICI Lombard needed one cross-platform digital app — without risking trust on a product handling live policies and claims.
Approach
Rebuilt the payment gateway into a centralized, policy-linked system with automated reconciliation, phased the GTM per feature, and ran behavior-based A/B tests on digital insurance cards and push notifications.
Impact
500K+ users onboarded in 6 months, policy conversion up 22% (~$4.2M+ in new premium), transaction failures down 18%.
04 Craft · HCI research

Designing for students who want to help, not search

Read the case study → Project deck ↗ Figma prototype ↗ Full process docs ↓
Problem
College students at Texas A&M wanted to volunteer but couldn't find opportunities that fit their schedule or transportation — EventVol, a full HCI research project.
Approach
Ran user interviews and affinity mapping, built personas and a journey map, then iterated mid-fidelity wireframes into a working Figma prototype with a full style guide.
Impact
Shipped a validated end-to-end prototype covering discovery, transportation coordination and feedback — proof of the research-to-prototype process, not just the output.
